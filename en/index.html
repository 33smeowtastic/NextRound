Keep interview preparation ready for the next rehearsal.

NextRound is a local-first, offline-capable workspace for personal interview preparation, including company applications and public-sector recruitment. The app interface is primarily Chinese; the open-source README provides complete Chinese and English instructions.

Open NextRound →

What is available

  • Organize roles, organizations, questions, answers, introductions and reusable content cards.
  • Practice questions at Heaven, Human and Hell difficulty, use timers and local recordings, and save manual self-ratings and reviews.
  • Connect actual interview questions with preparation records, trace feedback and compare opportunities using transparent rules.
  • Preview unified JSON before import and preserve duplicate/conflict boundaries. Full backups include recordings and attachments.

How AI and the app work together

  1. Download the training protocol and practice in an external AI tool of your choice.
  2. Ask that tool to produce structured JSON with the organization template. Review the content and remove information you do not want to share.
  3. Preview and confirm the import in NextRound, then rehearse, edit and review locally.

Where your data stays

Business records are stored in IndexedDB for the current browser origin. The app has no account, cloud sync, built-in model API, analytics or automatic upload of business records. Sharing materials with an external AI is your own action and is subject to that service's rules.

Clearing site data, using private browsing or changing devices can remove access to your records. Full JSON backups are not encrypted: keep them secure and export them regularly.

Current boundaries

There is no independent mastery algorithm or spaced repetition system; self-ratings are entered manually. Ritual is still under construction. Real phone sensors, real microphones and OS installation require checks in their target environments.
